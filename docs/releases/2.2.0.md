## 📼 A flight recorder for your laptop

When a laptop resets out of nowhere, Windows often keeps almost nothing. Now LaptopGuardian writes down temperatures, load, power and the busiest apps every 10 seconds, straight to disk, so the next crash comes with a black box: when it stopped, and what it was doing.

<img src="{{ASSETS}}/shutdowns.png" alt="A crash explained: reset itself without warning, with the last reading before it">

## ⚡ "Reset itself without warning"

A new verdict for the worst kind of crash: running one second, rebooting the next, no error. It tells you what that points to (heat, power, memory, hardware) and how to narrow it down.

## 🧭 Driver updates, walked through

Behind on your NVIDIA or AMD graphics driver, or your BIOS? LaptopGuardian tells you what you have, what's newest, and gives you numbered steps, then opens the right app for you: **NVIDIA App, AMD Software, Intel's checker, MyASUS, Armoury Crate,** or your laptop maker's tools.

<img src="{{ASSETS}}/drivers.png" alt="The Drivers page: numbered steps and a button that opens NVIDIA App">

## 🐛 Fixed

- **Crashes were blamed on sleep.** 2.1.0 misread gaps in Windows' battery report as sleep. Now only Windows' own sleep records count, and your existing crash history is corrected automatically. *If you changed your sleep settings because of that advice, you can change them back on the Power page.*
- **Crash times could be many minutes early.** It now uses the last thing known to have happened.
- **No more false heat warnings** on AMD and other cards that don't state a temperature limit.
- **Hibernate settings now stick** across every power plan, so performance-mode switches (such as Armoury Crate's) don't undo them.
- **"Clear old reports" now disappears** once the reports are gone.
- **No BIOS nagging** when you already have the newest your laptop's maker lists.
- **Hidden suggestions can be brought back** from Settings.
